Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Pipeline is a job application tracker. This page explains what information it stores, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we store

  • From Google Sign-In: your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Job-search data you enter: company, role, salary, application stage, notes, and job posting links for the applications you track.

Why we store it

Your Google name, email, and photo identify your account and personalize the app. Your job-search data is stored so the board and dashboard can show your applications back to you across sessions and devices.

Who it's shared with

Nobody. Google is used only to sign you in (via OAuth) — we don't share your data with any other third party.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

How to delete your data

Sign in to Pipeline and click Delete account in the header. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and every job application associated with it. You can also delete individual job entries at any time from the board without deleting your account.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
